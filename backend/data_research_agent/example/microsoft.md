# Microsoft: An Overview

This report provides a concise overview of Microsoft, tracing its evolution from its founding in 1975 to its current status as a technology giant.  We will explore key milestones, including its pivotal partnership with IBM and the development of groundbreaking products like MS-DOS and Windows.  The report will also examine Microsoft's diverse product portfolio, spanning software, hardware, and cloud services, highlighting its continuous innovation and adaptation in the ever-evolving technological landscape.


## Company Overview

Microsoft, founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen, is a multinational technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington [1, 3, 4]. Initially located in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the company relocated to Bellevue, Washington in 1979 [1]. Microsoft's early success stemmed from developing the operating system MS-DOS for IBM's personal computer in 1981 [3].  This partnership catapulted Microsoft into a market-leading position, setting the stage for its growth in the software industry [2].

Today, Microsoft is a trillion-dollar company offering a wide range of software and hardware products and services [3]. Its best-selling products include the Windows operating system, the Microsoft Office productivity suite, the Xbox gaming console, the Bing search engine, and the Microsoft Azure cloud services platform [1].  Microsoft also offers a suite of enterprise resource planning (ERP) and customer relationship management (CRM) applications as part of Dynamics 365 [7].  The company's continuous innovation and expansion into new domains like cloud computing, AI, and gaming have solidified its position as a technology giant [5, 6].
