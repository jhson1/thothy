# Apple Inc.

This report provides a concise overview of Apple Inc., tracing its evolution from its founding in 1976 to its current status as a global tech giant.  From the Apple I to the iPhone, Apple's innovative products and services have revolutionized personal computing and shaped modern technology. We will explore the company's history, mission, key product lines, and the impact of its relentless pursuit of user-friendly design and technological advancement.


## Company Overview

Apple Inc., formerly Apple Computer Company, was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne.[2, 3, 7] The company's initial focus was on marketing Wozniak's Apple I desktop computer.[2, 3]  Incorporated in Cupertino, California, on January 3, 1977,[2, 3] Apple quickly revolutionized personal computing with the introduction of the Apple II, featuring the first color graphics.[3]

Apple's mission is "to making the best products on earth, and to leaving the world better than we found it."[4] This reflects the company's long-standing dedication to innovation and user experience.  From its early personal computers like the Macintosh to the iPod, iPhone, and iPad, Apple has consistently introduced groundbreaking products.[5, 7]  These products are known for their simple design, user-friendly interfaces, and integration with Apple's software and services.[1, 5]

In 2007, reflecting its expansion beyond computers,  Apple dropped "Computer" from its name.[3] The company's product line now includes Mac computers, iPhones, iPads, Apple Watches, AirPods, Apple TVs, and various accessories.[6]  Apple also offers a suite of services, including iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple TV+.[5]  The company's focus on product innovation has made it a global leader in technology, and in 2018, Apple became the first publicly traded company to reach a market capitalization of $1 trillion.[3, 7]
